# Working rules for this repo

- **Do not commit or push by default.** Piotr commits himself, or explicitly asks for a commit. Never offer to commit as a routine follow-up.
- **Work directly on main. Piotr works alone here** — no feature branches, no PRs. So, when commiting provide a very thorough explanation of why and what for.


# Copy guideline

- **The site's job is to sell Piotr's services.** Every line on the homepage should help a buyer decide to get in touch: what he can do, how he works, and where his hard limits are.
- **Say it with confidence.** Honest and specific, but not self-deprecating. Leave out jokes at his own expense, failure-rate framing, and lines that talk the reader out of hiring him.
- **Hard boundaries stay short and plain.** State them as facts (e.g. no permanent roles, no people management), not as apologies or lists of demands.
- **Don't quote or paraphrase the essays in sales copy.** They show his voice; they aren't a source of taglines. Linking to them is fine.
- **Don't invent facts** (clients, numbers, outcomes, rates). Mark gaps as *TBD* or ask.
